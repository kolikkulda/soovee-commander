Soovee Commander 0.19.1 (test build)

Pairing
- Tap a nearby device and it shows its pairing code and QR code; type or scan it on the other device.
- On macOS the code window opens automatically when a phone or TV asks for it; the folder to share can be chosen while approving.
- Pairing again with a new code works when one side has forgotten the pairing; earlier folder permissions are revoked.
- iPhone and Android can remove a paired device; removing it also revokes its access to shared folders.
- Phones and TVs use their Wi-Fi address, not mobile data or VPN addresses.
- After a network address change, a paired device is found again by its identity and verified with its key.
